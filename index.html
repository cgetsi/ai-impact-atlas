Data Labels & Sources

How Numbers Are Verified

There is no single U.S. government registry mandating facility-by-facility electricity and water disclosure for private data centers. Every metric in this atlas is labeled as Reported, Estimated, or Undisclosed.

Reported

Publicly Disclosed

Published in an audited sustainability report, utility filing, or federal agency dataset.

Estimated

Modeled Figure

Derived by researchers (e.g., Epoch AI) from permits, satellite imagery, or disclosed MW.

Undisclosed

Not Published

Site location is verified, but the operator keeps power and water numbers private.

Federal Agencies & U.S. National Labs

  • Lawrence Berkeley National Lab (2024 DOE Report)

    Congressionally mandated DOE baseline for U.S. data center electricity (176 TWh in 2023; 325–580 TWh by 2028) and direct/indirect cooling water.

    eta.lbl.gov ↗
  • U.S. DOE / Pacific Northwest National Lab (PNNL) — IM3 Data Center Atlas

    Open-source U.S. Department of Energy spatial dataset mapping data center footprints and regional grid intersections (DOI: 10.57931/17054299).

    im3.pnnl.gov ↗
  • U.S. EPA — eGRID & ECHO (Air Permits)

    Official federal database of state/regional grid carbon-free generation mixes (eGRID) and industrial air permits for generators & turbines (ECHO).

    epa.gov/egrid ↗
  • U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA)

    Annual U.S. electricity consumption by residential, commercial, and industrial sectors (Electric Power Annual, RECS, MECS, Form EIA-923).

    eia.gov ↗
  • U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) — National Water Use

    Federal baseline for U.S. agricultural irrigation, municipal public supply, industrial, and thermoelectric power plant water withdrawals and consumption.

    usgs.gov ↗
  • USDA Economic Research Service & NREL

    Official U.S. Major Land Uses inventory (2.26B acres across pasture, forest, cropland, urban, and highways) and NREL utility-scale solar land baselines.

    ers.usda.gov ↗
